- Comparisons with the page ("ristiriita") are reliable in both conditions, with no invalid comments. The weak categories are language, tasks and culture, which are the areas a student persona is supposed to be good at.
- Most of the persona's taste verdicts come from one chapter: 6 of the 13 are in fi2 Kant.
- **Missed by both:** the verifier listed 40 problems (3 to 5 per chapter, 4.4 on average) that neither condition reported. By definition, none of the 40 was caught. Most are small unsourced claims, repeated reassurance, praise lines, opening content notes, and clumsy Finnish.
- I spot-checked four verifier calls against the inputs, and all four hold:
  - The "Ryhmanohjaaja" label typo is real.
  - "Kaikki työt tehdään" is odd Finnish.
  - fi4 changes the page's "tunnetun" to "vahvimman".
  - "samalta viivalta" is fine Finnish.
- One verifier call is overrated: the op1 "neljään osaan" line was judged a large defect, but it is a comma ambiguity. The page has "ihmiset, tekeminen" and the chapter has "ihmiset ja tekeminen". The severity labels in general are unreliable.

## 2. What each condition is good and bad at

**Persona: good at**
- Changed thresholds and definitions:
  - op1: the page says "useimpina päivinä yli kaksi viikkoa", and the chapter drops "useimpina päivinä", which lowers the threshold.
  - op1: the chapter's definition of wellbeing leaves out the page's "asioilla on merkitystä".
- Content the chapter drops: syrjintä, social loneliness, and task sentences d and e in te1.
- Student-side problems:
  - fi4: "Oikea vaihtoehto on joka kerta pisin ja tarkin" (the right answer can be guessed from its form). Valid.
  - fi2: "Sanat taipumus, täydellinen ja epätäydellinen velvollisuus tulevat ilman selitystä". Valid.
  - te2: "tarkoituksellisuus" was added to the OPH definition of bullying. Valid. Explicit missed it.

**Persona: bad at**
- It wants a single right answer, which the sites avoid on purpose:
  - op2: "Eemil kysyy, onko yrittäminen järkevää, eikä kysymykseen vastata". Invalid.
  - op1: "Kysymykseen ... ei saa vastausta" about "Niin voi pyytää". Invalid.
- It makes false claims about idiom: "Se todella riippuu alasta. Lause kuulostaa käännökseltä". Invalid.
- It misreads a normal choice menu as a loop (op1). Taste.

**Explicit: good at**
- Defects that need careful reading against the page:
  - op1: the chapter says four parts but lists three.
  - te1: "Yksinäisyyden syy-yhteys on käännetty päinvastaiseksi". Valid.
  - fi3: the stipend choice is a mix of two proposals that is not on the page. Valid.
  - fi4: the page marks "ei vähempää" as the site's own addition to Clifford, and the chapter drops that mark.
- House rules: help blocks in all four filosofia chapters, repeated reassurance, praise that marks one answer as right, and the te1 112 line repeated four times.

**Explicit: bad at**
- Its own culture label: in te2 it calls 112 "yhdysvaltalainen turvallisuusohje". Partly: 112 is the Finnish emergency number.
- False premises:
  - op2: "Europass ... esitetaan ilman selitystä", but the page does not explain it either. Invalid.
  - op2: other skill options "eivät saa vahvistusta". Invalid.
- Idiom: "Lähteä samalta viivalta on lähellä englannin ilmausta". Invalid.

## 3. Persona vs explicit

- **What the persona adds:** a handful of valid points that explicit missed:
  - guessable answer options (fi4)
  - unexplained terms (fi2)
  - the added bullying intent (te2)
  - a teacher who drops the fiction to talk about "the site" (te3; explicit also caught this)
  - tasks the novel solves for the student (te1 Noor map, fi1 Sanni, fi3 Mira)

  But explicit catches more house-rule breaches. Most of what the persona adds could be had by putting 3 or 4 student-view items into the explicit criteria list.
- **Finnish teenager voice:** this does not work. The persona's language comments are 44% precise, and its "sounds translated" claims are mostly wrong.
- **US drift:** mild, and in the direction the literature predicts:
  - In op1 it asks for crisis contacts: "Kriisiohje ei kerro, keneen tai mihin ottaa yhteyttä". The right fix is to delete the block, not to add contacts.
  - It repeatedly wants a closed answer to value questions.
  - It missed the help blocks in fi1 and fi4, which explicit flagged.
  - On the other side, it correctly called the te1 opening content note American.
  - It never asked for praise.
- **Overall:** the persona mostly duplicates explicit, adds a little noise and a few unique valid points. A 5-point precision gap on about 100 comments each is not strong evidence on its own. The direction matches Tao et al. 2024 and NormAd 2025.

## 4. Page as reference vs the brief 535 outsider reports (no page)

- **Only the page comparison finds:**
  - factual divergence: biopankki withdrawal "milloin tahansa" vs 12 §; the Kant second-formula reason; the Gorgias line put in Sokrates' mouth; "väärä" vs "valetasapuolisuus"
  - unsourced additions: Sekasin, Nettiturvakoti, Ohjaamo, "Käyttötapa ratkaisee"
  - dropped caveats ("sivuston oma jäsennys")
  - house-rule breaches (help blocks, reassurance, praise)
  - dropped tasks

  None of these appear in the outsider reports. That is expected, since the outsider was told to know no course material.
- **Only the outsider finds:**
  - references a reader cannot resolve: AA IV 421, Apologia 21d, 1131a, Finlex, ETENE, Työmarkkinatori, olkiukko, kultainen sääntö, objektivismi/relativismi, opiskeluhuolto vs opiskeluterveydenhuolto, hyvinvointialue
  - places a student cannot see (te3: "taululle piirretyt taulukot")

  The persona and explicit reviews raised only some of these (AA IV, täydellinen velvollisuus, hyvinvointialue, the fi2 Muuttoaamu reference).
- **Outsider weaknesses:**
  - All 9 reports ask where the "muistio" is. That is a site-wide convention, so it is noise per chapter.
  - The YES/NO verdict is unstable.
  - It pushes toward US habits. te1 asks for "Kriisipuhelimen numero ja aukioloajat" and the Sekasin address. te2 asks for Nettiturvakoti chat hours. op1 asks for Ohjaamo and Mielenterveystalo addresses. Its "need more" list rewards exactly the additions that the page check flags as unsourced.

## 5. Recommendation

1. **Run explicit with the page on all 88 chapters, using Haiku.** Haiku is enough for the first pass: 100% of quotes were found, page comparisons were 100% precise, and the cost is about 0.0025 USD per chapter. Drop the persona. Fold its useful parts into the criteria list:
   - unexplained terms and citation formats
   - fiction breaks (talk about "sivu" or "sivusto", credit lines)
   - tasks the novel answers for the student
   - answer options that can be guessed from length
   - promises the chapter does not keep
2. **Prompt changes:**
   - Require the page line next to every "ristiriita" or "puuttuu" comment.
   - Say explicitly: never ask for contact details, numbers or help info; never ask for a single right answer to a value question; 112 is Finnish.
   - Allow language comments only with a concrete Finnish alternative, and mark them as low confidence.
   - Define the severity levels.
   - Tell the reviewer to merge duplicate comments.
3. **Keep the outsider pass only as a cheap check for terms and references**, with a filter that drops requests for help info and addresses and drops "muistio" questions.
4. **Verification before a writer acts on a comment:**
   - A Sonnet verifier is required: check that the quote exists, check it against the page, and assign a verdict.
   - Writers act only on valid or partly comments, using the verifier's reason.
   - Drop taste and invalid comments.
   - Language comments, at 44% and 64% precision, need a Finnish human check.
   - The verifier's "missed" step found more than either reviewer (40 problems). Keep it, or test Sonnet as the reviewer on a few chapters. The reviewing step is where a stronger model may pay off.
5. **What the pilot cannot show:**
   - n = 9, one run per condition, so there is no variance estimate. The 80% vs 85% gap could be noise.
   - The verifier is itself a model, and it was apparently given the same house rules as the explicit prompt. That biases "valid" toward explicit.
   - Only four verifier calls were checked by hand, by me.
   - "Missed" is a lower bound, not full recall.
   - The pilot does not show whether writers' fixes improve the chapters, or how this behaves on non-health and non-philosophy subjects.
